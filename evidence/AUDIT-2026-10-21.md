# Independent publication reconciliation — 2026-10-21

## Scope and exact meaning of verification

This note reconciles the **public article** `index.html`, **evidence lab** `proofs.html`, the final editorial reports of 8 August 2026 and the prior **Phase 6** / **Phase 7** research audits.

**This review checks published text and historical reporting. It is not an independent rerun of historical code, binaries or archived messages.** References to A/B/C grades refer to evidence examined in the original research. The source binaries or old ZIP containers are not silently represented as present in this public repository.

### Historical corpus integrity: two different observations

- The final August report describes an earlier audit in which **8 of 9 historical container hashes matched**; historical S2's original ZIP was missing.
- The **Phase 7** audit report, run against a separate folder, found **0 of 9 containers physically present** and consequently **verified 0 hashes in that run**.
- Both statements must remain visible together. The repository does not distribute S1–S9. No new hash matches were obtained as part of this publication update.
- Only **7 of 10** historical S2 comparison metrics were reproduced under the explicitly documented normalization rules. Three older counts **7008/3774/2417** could not be exactly reproduced (alternative **7001/3781/2392** observed). Never conflate historic targets with reproduced outputs.

## Publication exclusions

Do **not** assert:
1. The complete Market was a fully working on-chain eBay.
2. Market removal immediately abolished all payment-by-IP or order protocol components.
3. r132 disabled the 15 Script opcodes.
4. A dedicated 1 MB block cap existed in the launch client.
5. CAlert could run arbitrary commands on other clients.
6. SMTP metadata, UTC offset, build DLLs, spelling or IRC nicknames prove Satoshi's physical identity.
7. The monetary overflow incident demonstrably caused Satoshi's departure.
8. Nonmatching archive Message-IDs prove forged, censored or secret correspondence.
9. The 2010–2018 IRC log is itself a transcript of Satoshi.
10. All nine containers are currently hosted or re-verified by this site.

- Links to hosted research resources may change. Public URLs are starting points, not proof of snapshot equivalence.

## Workflow for a community contribution

1. Identify claim ID in `evidence/CLAIMS.csv`.
2. Retrieve an independent primary source; record URL, date, file path, revision/commit, byte length, SHA-256.
3. Re-run the exact procedure and capture both expected and *actual* output.
4. Include a negative control; document failures instead of tweaking normalization to fit old counts.
5. Submit evidence in a pull request; keep claims qualified until corroboration.
6. Remove personal paths, addresses, API tokens and embedded metadata before submission.
